Robotics Engineer

Kayaa
Humanoid teleoperation & robot learning

  • VR teleoperation stack for the 29-DoF Unitree G1: Quest 3 controller and hand poses fused with ZED BODY_38 skeletons, GMR retargeting into NVIDIA SONIC whole-body control. 0.7 cm wrist accuracy on hardware.

  • Deployed on Jetson AGX Orin with custom SONIC and TensorRT builds, plus a stereo vision return path from the ZED to the Quest over WebRTC.

  • Cognitive architecture for context-aware surgical assistance, integrating LLM and RAG pipelines, with an STT, LLM and TTS stack running on the Orin itself.

  • Robot-learning stack: an imitation-learning data pipeline for GR00T N1.7 action-space fine-tuning on a new embodiment, and baseline locomotion policies across Isaac Sim, Isaac Gym and MuJoCo.
